Of the five orchestration platforms reviewed, two currently price below a $30/mo consumer tier — both do so by capping monthly credits and file uploads well under the category average, which shows up quickly for anyone doing sustained research or automation work rather than occasional chat.

The remaining three cluster between $32–$45/mo. The spread is mostly explained by two variables: context window size, and whether multi-model routing ships in the base tier or sits behind a paid add-on.
